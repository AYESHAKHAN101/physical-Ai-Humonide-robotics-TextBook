# Quickstart: Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)

## Overview
This module introduces NVIDIA Isaac, a robotics platform for AI-powered robots, focusing on perception, navigation, and learning capabilities.

## Chapter Structure
1. **NVIDIA Isaac Overview**: Understanding the Isaac ecosystem and its role in Physical AI
2. **Perception & Navigation**: Exploring perception and navigation pipelines in Isaac
3. **Sim-to-Real Transfer**: Understanding domain randomization and deployment to edge devices

## Learning Path
- Start with Chapter 1 to understand the Isaac ecosystem
- Proceed to Chapter 2 to learn about perception and navigation concepts
- Complete with Chapter 3 to understand sim-to-real transfer techniques

## Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of robotics concepts
- Familiarity with AI and machine learning concepts
- Knowledge of simulation environments (helpful but not required)
